# Home, Menu, and Orders visual concept

Open [index.html](index.html) locally and use the links to switch between Home, Menu, Profile, Orders, order detail, and Cart. Click a featured or menu dish to add it to the local sample cart. The desktop menu panel, header count, phone count, and cart view update immediately; clicking the same dish again increases its quantity. **Continue to checkout** opens a local checkout preview when the cart has an item. This standalone concept does not connect to Supabase, create orders, quote final charges, or submit payments. The customer module is unchanged.

Preview captures: [Home desktop](screenshots/home-desktop.png), [Menu with an item in the desktop cart](screenshots/menu-with-cart-desktop.png), [Checkout from desktop Menu](screenshots/checkout-from-menu-desktop.png), [Home after adding on phone](screenshots/home-added-phone.png), [Cart with items on phone](screenshots/cart-with-items-phone.png), [Checkout from phone Cart](screenshots/checkout-from-cart-phone.png), [Profile phone](screenshots/profile-phone.png), [Orders phone](screenshots/orders-phone.png), and [order detail phone](screenshots/order-detail-phone.png). The screenshots folder has the five core illustrated screens at 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×900, plus cart and checkout preview states.

The three sample dish names and prices come from the earlier local customer menu snapshot on 2026-11-03; they may now be stale. The subtotal in this concept is simple sample-item arithmetic, not RRJ's server quote or final payable amount. The local food images illustrate placement and require catalog image-path verification before use in the app. The Home section says **Featured dishes** because no trustworthy popularity ranking has been established. The Orders list and detail show labelled placeholders, not a real customer's orders or statuses.

Design decisions shown: food on Home, search and categories on Menu, a desktop cart beside the menu, a separate mobile Cart destination, and Orders reached through Profile. An Orders list opens a detail where the existing tracking and payment follow-up would remain.
