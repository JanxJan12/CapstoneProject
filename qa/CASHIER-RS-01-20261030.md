# CASHIER-RS-01 — Receipts & Sales investigation

**Date:** 30 October 2026 (Asia/Manila). **Scope:** read-only architecture, financial, workflow, security, and UI audit of the active Cashier view. **Decision:** retain the specialized receipt audit workspace; simplify its routine surface and correct misleading derived presentation in a later, separately authorized RS-02. No application, migration, RPC, RLS, schema, or hosted business record was changed.

## Executive summary

Receipts & Sales has a legitimate Cashier purpose: find an earlier sale, confirm it was recorded, answer a customer payment question, reprint its receipt, and follow its linked operational order. Orders tracks fulfillment and operational action; Shift Settlement reconciles one drawer; Manager Reports analyzes restaurant-wide, successfully fulfilled revenue. Those purposes overlap in identifiers and amounts, but not in authority or time axis.

The active history list and CSV use the deployed `get_cashier_transaction_history` RPC, scoped to the signed-in active Cashier across shifts. A selected row uses `get_cashier_sale` for item/payment/status detail. Source and current read-only hosted catalog checks support the access boundary. Current authenticated Cashier A/B access, actual browser workflow, and print remain untested. The largest confirmed presentation weakness is **More sales details**: “Most sold item” and “Average basket” use whatever matching orders happen to reside in the operational provider, not the complete historical page. The detail audit also labels a payment verification with the current Cashier's display name instead of the recorded verifier ID, and the detail basket displays unit price × quantity even though the stored line total is available. These are source-confirmed risks of misleading historical presentation, not observed hosted financial corruption.

## Active architecture and data path

`CashierModule` routes `/cashier/orders-receipts/receipts` to `OrdersReceiptsPage` and its active `TransactionHistoryPage` tab. The Orders tab has its own operational page. History's hook calls `get_cashier_transaction_history(p_limit=50,p_offset,p_filters)` for the list and `cashier_shifts` for the current Cashier's shift/terminal choices. It maps each returned row to a `Transaction`, uses a selected row's exact order UUID to call `get_cashier_sale`, and checks transaction, cashier, payment, and shift links before opening detail or receipt. `ReceiptDialog`/`ReceiptContent`/`prepareReceipt` are shared with POS and Dashboard. The CSV fetches successive pages of the *same RPC filter* in blocks of 100, capped at 5,000, rather than serially hydrating every sale. The list is 50 rows per page, ordered by `created_at DESC, id DESC` in the RPC.

The pre-IA lineage report describes an older current-shift-only screen and separate export path. That was corrected by DATA-01 and deployed in DATA-01D. It is provenance, **not** the active route. `transactionExport.ts` remains in source as a pure legacy adapter/test helper; the active History page imports only its export gate, not its old read/export path. Opening Orders does not mount History until that tab is visited; visited tabs remain mounted and retain filters.

## Workspace comparison and target workflow

**Orders** remains the live operational projection: fulfillment status, payment state, customer service detail, allowed edits/cancellation/release, and order timeline. Receipts & Sales starts from an immutable sale/receipt identifier and provides recorded payment evidence and reprint. Search and order linkage overlap legitimately. Keep the exact IA-01 bidirectional links. A receipt can be accessible only to its owner even when Orders shows a staff-visible order; do not widen ownership to make a link always work.

**Shift Settlement** answers “what should be in my assigned drawer now?” It uses one open shift's opening cash, Cash receipts, and server finalization, while History can span closed shifts and include GCash. The History page's Cash sum is neither opening plus collections nor a recorded cash-movement ledger. Keep reconciliation, variance, close, and historical settlement in Shift Settlement; add plain scope wording in RS-02.

**Manager Reports** answers restaurant-wide performance by successful fulfillment and verified payment. It owns trends, daily totals, and top-item analytics. The Cashier still needs own-sale evidence and occasional CSV. Page-level “most sold item” is weak operationally and can be misleading because of incomplete item linkage; do not promote it as a performance metric. Manager refund authorization stays in Manager workflows.

Recommended routine path: enter a receipt, transaction, or order number → see concise, owner-scoped matches with customer/date/amount/method/status → open transaction evidence → preview/reprint the completed receipt or follow its linked order. Keep date/method/status and advanced customer/shift/terminal filters one step away. Show a page-scoped total only with explicit wording. Keep audit IDs and uncommon breakdowns inside details. This is a bounded workflow refinement, not a wholesale redesign.

## Retrieval, security, and integrity findings

- The current RPC checks an active Cashier profile, `tx.cashier_id = auth.uid()`, and the joined shift owner; it rejects `NULL` or out-of-range page parameters, applies filters before count/page, and sorts by transaction creation time plus UUID. The frontend also validates owner/status/links, but server predicates are the authority. Current hosted catalog read confirmed definer mode, empty search path, both owner predicates, active-profile check, stable order, `anon` execute denied, and authenticated execute granted. This does not prove two actual Cashier accounts cannot cross-read; signed-in A/B runtime remains open.
- `get_cashier_sale` is called once on selection, not per list row; it requires own-sale access. Detail checks cashier, transaction, payment, and shift IDs against the list row. Order navigation requires both exact human and database order IDs in operational state. No source path here grants Cashier general direct payment-row access.
- `fetchHistoryShifts` is called in the same `Promise.all` on **every** filter/page request and requests up to 500 own shifts. This is confirmed redundant network work and a possible option-completeness problem only above that cap. The shared Cashier provider still fetches operational state; its traffic is not caused solely by opening History.
- The active CSV repeats the same server filters and validates page counts and ownership. Offset pagination without a stable snapshot can still duplicate/skip rows if records move while exporting with unchanged total. This is a concurrency risk, not a reproduced loss. The 5,000 export cap is explicit; the RPC offset ceiling is 10,000.
- Search uses broad `ILIKE` on several identifiers and GCash reference; no performance timing or query plan was measured. Index work is **deferred** until history volume and latency warrant it. The one-choice Cashier UI filter is ignored by the request mapper, but ownership remains correctly enforced server-side.
- The selected detail's payment verification timeline takes `transaction.cashierName` (current session display name) rather than `payment.verifiedBy` even though hydration carries that ID. This can misattribute a historical verification if names change. The transaction actor name is similarly session-sourced; no arbitrary profile lookup should be added without authorization.
- `TransactionTable` renders a screen-reader-only masked contact from `state.orders`; it is not needed for routine receipt identification and may convey “No linked contact” for history not present in that operational cache. This is a minor presentation issue, not evidence of a data leak.
- Historical receipt printing has a strict recorded-data guard. Inconsistent legacy snapshots can be blocked rather than printed falsely. The local synthetic 80 mm PDF does not establish actual signed-in/physical print.

## Prioritized decisions and RS-02 proposal

| Priority | Status | Decision and proposed RS-02 boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | No current defect demonstrated | Preserve deployed owner checks, sale/payment links, transaction and refund contracts. Do not reclassify History amounts as net/drawer or alter RPCs from this audit. |
| P1 | Open acceptance | Test real Cashier A/B isolation, closed-shift detail/reprint, CSV ownership, and live print with an authenticated browser. Investigate any failure before changing authority. |
| P2 | Confirmed source presentation issue | Remove or qualify incomplete basket/item rankings in More sales details; show stored line totals in detail; avoid current-session attribution for historical verification. Keep recorded data and existing RPCs. |
| P2 | Confirmed source inefficiency | Fetch own shift choices independently of page/filter changes; make 500-choice limit explicit or support bounded search if actual shift volume needs it. |
| P2 | Improvement, not proven defect | Make order-number lookup routine; clarify visible-page amount versus drawer/Manager revenue; refine empty/error/retry and export concurrency only after browser and volume evidence. An RPC search change needs separate review. |
| P3 | Confirmed redundant control | Remove the one-choice Cashier filter UI; retain ownership enforcement. Hide zero-value detail discount and needless accessibility-only contact fallback after interaction review. |
| Deferred | Policy/data contract | Net refunds, payout drawer attribution, cross-Cashier lookup, restaurant-wide analytics, and 58 mm receipt support require separate authority/evidence. No source or hosted record should be deleted for this milestone. |

RS-02 should be **Cashier frontend-only** for the confirmed display/attribution/filter simplifications, with focused local tests and authenticated UX acceptance. Any RPC search/export change, refund financial definition, or cross-role access must be separately scoped and reviewed. Do not change Orders, Shift Settlement, Manager Reports, or the existing financial formulas merely to reduce visual overlap.
