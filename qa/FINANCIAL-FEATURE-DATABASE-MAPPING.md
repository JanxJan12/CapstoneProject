## Authority and join path

`orders.id` → `payments.order_id` → `cashier_transactions.payment_id` and `cashier_transactions.order_id` → `cashier_shifts.id`. `paid_order_cancellations` retains the three original IDs. Fulfillment is `orders.completed_at` for non-delivery or a delivered `delivery_assignments.delivered_at` for delivery. `order_items` provides sold-time item identity and pricing; current `menu_items.price` does not restate a historical sale. Inventory issuance/restoration is separate from financial collection.

The Manager reporting RPC first finds successful fulfillment, then groups verified payments **per order** before summing by day. This avoids multiplying payment amount by order-item or assignment counts. It excludes pending, rejected and voided payments, unfulfilled paid orders, and cancellations. `total_orders` counts fulfilled orders even if there is no verified payment; `average_order_value` uses the count of fulfilled orders that do have verified payments. That distinction is visible in the RPC and requires an owner decision on whether the label should say “average verified fulfilled order.”

## Provenance and limits

The isolated financial fixture uses synthetic identities and no real customer details, proofs or receipts. Its direct status seeds test reporting projections; separate RPC sequences test POS, payment transition and cancellation contracts. Directly seeded completed orders lack `order_items`, so their report `top_item_name = null` is fixture-limited and is not a product defect. The local diagnostic chain has 52 versions and is not a successful original-only chronological replay. Hosted read-only observations from earlier reports are not represented as H-07 live acceptance.
